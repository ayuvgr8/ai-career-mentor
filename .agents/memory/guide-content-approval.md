---
name: Guide content approval
description: Content-owner approval boundary for career guide draft labels.
---
Keep a guide's draft label until the content owner explicitly approves its copy and detail level.

**Why:** The user required an explicit review gate, and approved the existing front-end engineering, performance marketing, product design, and core product management copy on 2026-10-24. That approval does not extend to other guides or substantively revised copy.

**How to apply:** Retain realistic schedule and learning-time caveats after approval. Seek renewed approval for substantive content changes rather than treating approval of existing copy as blanket permission.

New optional practice suggestions are reviewed independently from the existing guide copy; keep them labeled as draft suggestions even inside an approved guide.

**Why:** Approval of the original guide does not approve later additions, and adding an exercise should not revoke approval of unchanged copy.

**How to apply:** Preserve approved sections when adding practice content. Only remove a suggestion's draft label after explicit content-owner review of that suggestion.
